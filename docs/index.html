The right document. The wrong decision.

Finding a document that looks like the question is easy. The hard part is knowing which one decides it. A model can quote yesterday's decision perfectly, and you have nothing to show what it was given.

TLDR-G gives you the answer and three ways to check it.

Three checks on every answer

Know what matters

Every answer lists the passages it was given. One click opens each one at its source, in the exact document.

See what changed

When a decision was later reversed, you read the line that reversed it, highlighted in the document itself.

Prove why

Each answer gets a signed receipt of the question and its evidence, made before any model is asked. Anyone can verify it offline.

Verify the receipt yourself

The receipt proves what went in. Whether the answer is right stays your reviewers' call. The verifier is open source and needs no server and no account, so you can check our work without trusting us.

Try it on one workflow of your own

Bring one set of documents you need to get right: contracts, engineering specifications, policies, a case file. TLDR-G is a desktop app for you and your documents.

It runs on your hardware, and the app shows on every page where your evidence is sent. Answers come from a local model or a cloud model you choose. Or take just the evidence, with no model at all.
