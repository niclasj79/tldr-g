How we build it

Free engine. Open SDK. Not open-core.

The engine is free to run, distributed as a compiled binary. The SDK (the integration contracts and the verification surface) is open source under Apache-2.0, so you can build against a stable boundary and verify everything for yourself. The engine stays closed; the contracts you depend on stay open.

Improving it without your data

We are building an opt-in, content-free signal about how the engine selected its evidence: never your data, never one user exposed to another. It is off by default, inspectable, and will not switch on until its privacy guarantee is provable.

Open the hood

The visual demo runs in your browser and draws a corpus as terrain you can descend, to show the architecture at work. Its corpus is synthetic, and every figure on screen is a design concept, not a benchmark.
