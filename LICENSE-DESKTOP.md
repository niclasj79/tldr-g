1. **What you get.** TLDR-G Desktop — the compiled engine and its Cockpit — free of charge.
2. **Who may use it.** An individual, on machines they control, for any purpose including their work. Install it on as many of your own machines as you like.
3. **What it is not for.** Serving TLDR-G to other people, or operating it as a service on behalf of an organization — a shared server, a team deployment, an embedded service. That is TLDR-G Server, licensed separately.
4. **What the app does not do.** No activation and no licence check that phones home; no telemetry by default. An optional, content-free signal about *how* the engine rendered — never your data — is designed and stays **off unless you turn it on**; nothing switches on without your action. The app contacts no server of ours. On first run it downloads open model weights from Hugging Face unless you install the offline pack; nothing of yours is sent anywhere.
5. **What stays open.** The integration contracts and the verifier are Apache-2.0 in the public repository. This licence covers only the compiled engine and Cockpit.
